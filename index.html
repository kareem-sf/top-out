How to play

Watch the crane behind this card. That is the whole game.

  1. 1
    Tap to drop. The slab slides in. Tap (or press space) to drop it on the tower.
  2. 2
    Overhang gets cut. Whatever hangs over the edge falls off. Your next slab is only as big as what landed.
  3. 3
    Perfect drops grow it back. Land within 25 cm and it snaps. Three in a row and the slab grows.
  4. 4
    Pass inspections. Every 8 floors the consultant asks which material costs more. Right answer: a variation order and a safety net.
